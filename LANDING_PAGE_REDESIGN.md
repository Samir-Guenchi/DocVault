## Technical Details

### Colors
- **Primary Blue:** #0066cc
- **Dark Blue:** #0052a3
- **Text Dark:** #1a1a1a
- **Text Gray:** #6b7280
- **Border Gray:** #e5e7eb
- **Background Gray:** #f9fafb
- **Success Green:** #10b981
- **Footer Dark:** #1a1a1a

### Typography
- **Font Family:** System fonts (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, 'Segoe UI', Roboto)
- **Headings:** 800 weight, tight letter-spacing
- **Body:** 400-600 weight, 1.6-1.7 line-height
- **Sizes:** 14px-52px range

### Spacing System
- **Small:** 8px, 12px, 16px
- **Medium:** 24px, 32px
- **Large:** 48px, 60px, 80px

### Components
- **Buttons:** 10px border-radius, 14-16px padding
- **Cards:** 12-16px border-radius, subtle shadows
- **Icons:** 20-24px size, consistent stroke width
